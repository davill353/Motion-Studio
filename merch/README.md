# The AI Pound merch

Print-ready artwork for a print-on-demand clothing line. Navy garments with mint and white ink, matching the brand.

| # | File | Product | Placement | Print size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `print-files/01-left-chest-mark.png` | Tee, hoodie, sweatshirt | Left chest | ~4 × 4 in |
| 2 | `print-files/02-back-large.png` | Tee, hoodie | Full back | 15 × 18 in |
| 3 | `print-files/03-front-ai-writes-it-dark.png` | Navy/black tee | Front | 15 × 18 in |
| 4 | `print-files/04-front-ai-writes-it-light.png` | White/grey tee | Front | 15 × 18 in |
| 5 | `print-files/05-front-made-in-sandy.png` | Navy tee | Front | 15 × 18 in |
| 6 | `print-files/06-cap-embroidery.png` | Cap / beanie | Front, embroidered | ~4.5 × 1.75 in |

All files are transparent PNGs at 300 dpi for their print size. Edit `src/designs.py`, then run `python3 designs.py && node render.js` (and `node mockups.js` for the preview sheet).

**Brand colours:** navy `#16233b`, mint `#6ee7a8` (on light garments use darker green `#1d9a62` for contrast).
**Best combos:** set 1 + 2 together (chest front, big back) as the staff/team tee; 3 and 5 as public slogan tees.
