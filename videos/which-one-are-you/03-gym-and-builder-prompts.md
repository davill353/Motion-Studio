# "Which one are you?" Ep 3 & 4: Gym and Builder, Veo prompts

Same method as before: make shot 1, then **extend** it with shots 2, 3, 4 (same person throughout). Then a **new chat** for the calm side. Send only the longest file of each (4 files in total).
Landscape 16:9, person in the middle of the frame. No text, no logos. Garbled phone screens are fine (I replace them).

## 🏋️ GYM: chaos side (A)
**A1:** Realistic video, 16:9. A small, busy UK independent gym at 6pm. A muscular male personal trainer in his 30s, wearing a black gym T-shirt, is spotting a client on the bench press while his phone on the floor beside the bench keeps lighting up and buzzing. He keeps glancing down at it, distracted. Handheld camera, harsh overhead gym lighting. Sound: clanking weights, gym music, phone vibrating on the floor. No text, no logos.

**A2:** Continue the scene. The trainer stops the session, crouches down and types on his phone with both thumbs while his client sits waiting on the bench, holding the weights and looking annoyed. Handheld. Sound: gym music, client sighing, phone keyboard taps. No text, no logos.

**A3:** Continue the scene. At the small gym reception desk, the same trainer is on the phone while a queue of three people waits, one holding a membership leaflet and checking his watch. The desk is messy with paper forms. Handheld, harsh light. Sound: phone ringing, impatient murmurs. No text, no logos.

**A4:** Continue the scene. Late at night, the same trainer sits alone on a weight bench in the empty, half-lit gym, scrolling through dozens of messages on his phone, rubbing his forehead, exhausted. Moody light, slow push-in. Sound: quiet hum, notification pings. No text, no logos.

## 🏋️ GYM: calm side (B), new chat
**B1:** Realistic video, 16:9. A bright, modern UK boutique gym at 6pm. A friendly female personal trainer in her 30s with a ponytail, wearing a green gym top, coaches a client through squats, smiling and fully focused. Her phone on a shelf lights up once and goes dark; she doesn't need to look. Steady camera, bright natural light. Sound: upbeat gym music, encouraging coaching. No text, no logos.

**B2:** Continue the scene, close-up. The phone on the shelf shows a new message; the screen glows softly, then a second notification appears and it dims. In the blurred background the trainer high-fives her client. Steady camera, shallow depth of field. Sound: soft chime, gym music. No text, no logos.

**B3:** Continue the scene. At a tidy reception desk, a new member is welcomed by the same trainer with a smile and a handshake; a tablet on the desk shows a booking. Calm, bright, steady camera. Sound: friendly chatter. No text, no logos.

**B4:** Continue the scene. Evening at home, the same trainer relaxes on a sofa with a protein shake, glances at her phone, taps it once with a smile, and puts it down. Warm lamp light, steady camera. Sound: quiet room, one soft chime. No text, no logos.

## 🔨 BUILDER: chaos side (A)
**A1:** Realistic video, 16:9. A UK building site on a grey day. A male builder in his 40s in a hi-vis vest and hard hat is up a ladder fixing a roof gutter, holding tools in both hands. His phone rings in his pocket and he can't reach it. Handheld camera, natural overcast light. Sound: wind, drilling nearby, phone ringing. No text, no logos.

**A2:** Continue the scene. The builder climbs down, pulls off a glove with his teeth and fumbles for his phone, but the call has already ended. He looks at the screen, frustrated: missed call. Handheld, close. Sound: wind, frustrated sigh, voicemail beep. No text, no logos.

**A3:** Continue the scene. Sitting in the cab of his white van with the door open, the same builder plays a voicemail on speaker, then calls back; he listens, his face drops as the customer has already chosen someone else, and he hangs up and slumps. Handheld, overcast light. Sound: rain on the van roof, muffled phone voice. No text, no logos.

**A4:** Continue the scene. 10pm at a kitchen table, the same builder, still in work clothes, writes quotes by hand on paper surrounded by invoices and a cold cup of tea, rubbing his eyes, exhausted. Single lamp, moody light, slow push-in. Sound: clock ticking, pen scratching. No text, no logos.

## 🔨 BUILDER: calm side (B), new chat
**B1:** Realistic video, 16:9. A UK building site on a bright day. A female builder in her 30s in a hi-vis vest and hard hat lays bricks confidently on a new wall. Her phone in her chest pocket buzzes once and goes quiet, and she carries on working. Steady camera, sunny natural light. Sound: trowel on brick, birds, one short buzz. No text, no logos.

**B2:** Continue the scene. On her lunch break, the same builder sits on the tailgate of her van, looks at her phone, smiles, and taps it once with her thumb. Steady camera, close-up on her face and phone. Sound: birds, light breeze, soft chime. No text, no logos.

**B3:** Continue the scene. The same builder arrives at a smart house and is greeted at the front door by a happy homeowner holding a mug of tea; they shake hands and look at the garden together. Bright, steady camera. Sound: friendly chatter, birds. No text, no logos.

**B4:** Continue the scene. Evening, the same builder relaxes at home on the sofa with her feet up and a cup of tea, glances at her phone, taps it once with a small smile and puts it down. Warm lamp light, steady camera. Sound: quiet room, one soft chime. No text, no logos.
