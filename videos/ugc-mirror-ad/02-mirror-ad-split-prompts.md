# UGC lane: "The Mirror Ad", variant B (split screen)

Second version of the Mirror Ad, built on the LatchCall pattern David sent on 19 Nov 2026:
a problem-and-fix split screen that reads in one second with the sound off, ending on the product.
28 seconds, vertical 9:16, top/bottom split (top = the hard way, bottom = our way).

Reference: LatchCall "AI virtual receptionist for plumbers & handymen" (screen recording, 35s, 1290x2796).
What it does well: the pain is visible before anyone speaks, and the close names the three benefits
(answers calls, books appointments, delivers the job). What it lacks: no story, no reason to comment.

## Hard rules for this one
- Opening frame carries the search phrase **"AI UGC ads for skin clinics"** for the pre-post checker.
- No prices anywhere. CTA is "Message us to get yours".
- No before/after and no efficacy claims.
- No real clinic, staff, shopfront or client on camera. All generated or our own footage.
- Must stay visibly different from "Two Salons": different split (top/bottom here, not left/right),
  different rhythm (cuts every 1.5-2s, no held shots), different close (a question, not a logo).
- Label the finished video "AI-GENERATED SCENES · DEMO CONVERSATION" as the others do.

## Shot list (top = the old way, bottom = our way)

**T1 (0-3s) Top: the setup.** Realistic video, 16:9. A bright skin clinic room with a lighting stand,
a softbox and a tripod in shot, cables taped to the floor. No people. Slightly too bright, clinical.
Steady camera. Sound: softbox hum, someone off-camera saying "can we go again?" No text, no logos.

**B1 (0-3s) Bottom: the same room, calm.** Realistic video, 16:9. The same style of treatment room,
empty, tidy, morning light, nothing but the couch and towels. Steady camera. Sound: quiet room tone.
No text, no logos.

**T2 (3-10s) Top: the shoot.** Realistic video, 16:9. A woman in her 30s sits awkwardly on the clinic
couch while a man with a clipboard directs her, and a second person holds a phone on a gimbal. She
laughs nervously and looks at the lens. Slightly harsh light. Handheld. Sound: overlapping voices,
"chin down", "again". No text, no logos.

**T3 (5-8s) Top, insert.** Close-up, 16:9. A hand tapping a phone screen showing a long list of
unnamed video files with a clock in the corner. Shallow depth of field. No text, no logos.

**B2 (3-10s) Bottom: the other way.** Realistic video, 16:9. A clinic owner in her 40s sits at a small
desk with a cup of tea, scrolling her phone, unhurried, and smiles once at the screen. Soft daylight,
steady camera. Sound: room tone, one soft notification chime. No text, no logos.

**B3 (8-12s) Bottom, insert.** Close-up, 9:16 within the half. A phone screen in her hand, thumb
scrolling through three vertical video thumbnails, each one a person talking to camera. Soft light.
No readable text, no logos.

**T4 (10-13s) Top: the aftermath.** Realistic video, 16:9. The same woman from T2 sits alone on the
couch in an empty room, hair flat, checking her watch. Cool light. Steady camera. Sound: silence,
one sigh. No text, no logos.

**B4 (13-19s) Bottom: posting.** Realistic video, 16:9. The clinic owner taps her phone twice, sets it
down, and goes back to her tea. Steady camera, warm daylight. Sound: two soft taps, room tone.
No text, no logos.

**B5 (19-24s) Bottom: the payoff.** Realistic video, 16:9. Close-up of the phone on the desk as three
notification banners stack up one after another. Soft light, shallow depth of field. Sound: three soft
chimes, then a quiet, satisfied "mm". No readable text, no logos.

**End card (24-28s).** Built in the edit: navy, mint rule, the question, then the CTA.
