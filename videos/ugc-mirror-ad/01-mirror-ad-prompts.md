# UGC lane: "The Mirror Ad"

A UGC ad that sells our UGC ads, aimed at skin clinics. 22 seconds, vertical 9:16.
No prices anywhere (standing rule). CTA is "Message us to get yours".

## Hard rules for this one
- Opening frame must carry the search phrase **"AI UGC ads for skin clinics"** so the pre-post checker passes.
- No before/after imagery and no efficacy claims: the whole point is that clinics can't lean on those.
- Never show a real clinic's name, shopfront, staff or client. All footage is generated or our own.
- No price, no "from £x", no free-month offer (that waits until December/January if we want it).

## Voiceover (David's own voice, recorded or cloned)

1. (0-3s) "This isn't your clinic's ad. It's what your clinic's ad could look like."
2. (5-11s) "Real formats. Real voices. Made for clinics, not for everyone."
3. (11-17s) "You don't need a camera crew or a model. You need three videos a month that sound like a person, not a leaflet."
4. (17-22s) "Message us and we'll make yours."

## On-screen text
- 0-3s: `AI UGC ads for skin clinics` (top, small, mint) + `This isn't your clinic's ad.` (centre, large, white on navy)
- 3-11s: `Made for skin clinics` (lower third, mint)
- 11-17s: `No crew. No model. No filter.`
- 17-22s end card: navy background, mint rule, `Message us to get yours.`

## Shot list

**S1 (0-3s) Hook, generated.**
Realistic video, 9:16. A calm, bright skin clinic treatment room in the UK: white couch, folded towels, a small tray of unbranded serum bottles, soft daylight through a blind. No people, no logos, no text. Steady camera, slow push-in. Sound: quiet room tone.

**S2-S4 (3-11s) The proof, three cuts, about 2.5s each. These are the three UGC formats we sell.**
- **S2 "Talking head":** Realistic video, 9:16. A woman in her early 30s, natural skin, no heavy makeup, sits in a bright room and talks to camera like she's recommending something to a friend. Handheld, natural light. Sound: clean room tone. No text, no logos.
- **S3 "Product in hand":** Realistic video, 9:16, close-up. A hand lifts an unbranded white serum bottle from a marble bathroom shelf into soft window light, turns it once. Shallow depth of field. No text, no labels.
- **S4 "Routine":** Realistic video, 9:16. Over-the-shoulder shot of someone patting serum into their cheek at a bathroom mirror, morning light, calm and unhurried. No text, no logos.

**S5 (11-17s) The problem, generated or our own footage.**
Realistic video, 9:16. A clinic owner in her 40s sits at a small reception desk with a phone on a tripod pointing at her, looking awkward and unsure, then lowers her eyes. Slightly cool light. Sound: quiet room, one awkward cough. No text, no logos.

**S6 (17-22s) End card.** Built in the edit, not generated: navy #16233B, mint #6EE7A8 rule, "Message us to get yours."
