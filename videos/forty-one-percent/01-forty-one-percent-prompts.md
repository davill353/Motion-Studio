# "Forty-one percent"

A The AI Pound opinion video, built off Daniel Bell's INOVAITIQ post (21 Nov 2026) about the
Air IT Group survey: 41% of UK businesses brought in AI tools before agreeing what problem to solve.
His post is text. This is the video version, in David's own voice, aimed at clinics and salons.

32 seconds, vertical 9:16. Talking head plus cutaways. No prices. CTA is a message, not a purchase.

## Hard rules
- Opening frame carries the search phrase **"AI for small business"** for the pre-post checker.
- No prices, no "from £x", no free-month offer.
- No real client, staff member or shopfront on camera. David only, plus generated cutaways.
- Any generated scene is labelled "AI-generated scenes".
- Say the number out loud early. It is the whole hook.

## Voiceover (David's own voice)

1. (0-4s) "Forty-one percent of UK businesses bought AI before they worked out what problem it was for."
2. (4-10s) "I see it in clinics and salons all the time. The chatbot gets bought first. Then someone asks it for a price and it hasn't got a clue, because nobody wrote the prices down."
3. (10-18s) "The tool was never the hard part. The hard part is the stuff that only lives in your head. What you charge. What you refuse to do. Who gets bumped up the list when you're full."
4. (18-26s) "So here's the free version. For one week, every time someone asks you something, write the question and your answer in your notes. That's your knowledge base. That's what an assistant actually runs on."
5. (26-32s) "Do that before you buy anything. Message us if you want the version that answers properly."

## Shot list

**S1 (0-4s) Hook.** David to camera, close, daylight, plain navy or neutral wall. Straight in, no music sting. Hard cut, no fade.

**S2 (4-6s) Cutaway, generated.** Realistic video, 9:16. A small UK clinic reception desk, empty, a phone screen glowing with a chat window nobody is looking at. No people, no logos, no readable text. Steady camera. Label "AI-generated scenes" on this cut.

**S3 (6-10s) Back to David.** Same framing as S1, slightly closer. He says the price line as if it's a familiar annoyance, not a joke.

**S4 (10-14s) Cutaway, generated.** Realistic video, 9:16. Close-up of a handwritten price list on a clipboard, blurred so nothing is readable, a pen resting on it. Soft light.

**S5 (14-18s) Back to David.** Wider than S1, hands in frame, counting the three things off on his fingers.

**S6 (18-26s) The free tip.** David holds up his phone showing a plain notes app with a list of questions, screen angled away from readable text. Then cut to the same notes list, top down, text unreadable.

**S7 (26-32s) Close.** David to camera, calm, no hard sell. End card built in the edit.

## Why this one travels
The number does the work in the first second. The admission that clinics and salons get it wrong makes owners recognise themselves, which is what earns comments rather than likes. And the free tip gives value before any ask, which is the only reason a stranger watches to the end.

## Difference from Daniel's post
He sells an AI receptionist trained on your business. We sell the same build with an owner in the loop: the AI drafts, a person approves. That is the line to hold if anyone compares the two, and it belongs in the comments, not the video.
