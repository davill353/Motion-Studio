# Video audit, 17 November 2026

Every video in the repo was audited, not just the newest build. Trigger: the 16 November
brand film went out with four ElevenLabs takes mixed from 0.00s, so several voices read over
each other. It passed the commit check and the caption check and still had to be pulled from
YouTube, Facebook and Instagram. A checker that cannot see that fault is not a checker.

## What was checked

| Check | Method | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Committed | `git log -1` per file | 49 / 49 committed |
| Pushed | compared each commit against `origin/main` via `git ls-remote` | 49 / 49 on origin/main |
| Container | ffprobe: resolution, duration, audio codec | all as expected |
| Loudness | EBU R128 (`loudnorm` measurement pass) | 2 too quiet, both fixed |
| One voice, in order | 29 files transcribed end to end and read | 29 / 29 single voice, in order |
| Take cue positions | alignment of each take against the mix | brand film: 0.62 / 6.71 / 12.45 / 21.24s |
| References | every `renders/…mp4` path named in a calendar, post or automation | 29 missing, 20 silent |

## The fault, fixed

`renders/brand/ai-pound-brand-film-9x16.mp4` mixed all four takes at 0.00s. Rebuilt with an
`adelay` per take so the lines cue at 0.62, 6.71, 12.45 and 21.24 seconds, matching the card
changes. Re-transcribed: the four lines read in order, one voice. Committed `e4ad6df`, pushed.

`prepost_check.py` now aligns every take against the finished mix and blocks if two share a
cue. Tested against a deliberately stacked mix (blocked) and the corrected render (passed).

## Open items, deliberately not changed

1. **29 calendar paths do not exist.** `content-calendar-30-day-plan.csv` schedules
   `renders/plan/plan02.mp4` through `plan30.mp4`. Only plan01 variants are built. Any row
   that reaches `ready` will fail at publish. The calendar is a plan, not a build.
2. **20 committed videos have no audio track.** All are superseded drafts with a voiced twin
   (`ai-pound-shorts/*` silent next to `ai-pound-shorts-vo/*`), or silent by design
   (summit stories, case studies, the 3s hello-studio). Two are worth a decision:
   `renders/ads/ad-clinic-pilot-01.mp4` silent beside a voiced `-02`, and
   `public/builtlive/test-clinic-receptionist.mp4` silent beside a voiced `renders/builtlive`
   copy. Neither is embedded on the site and neither is referenced by a calendar row.
3. **Six families have no builder committed**, so they cannot be re-rendered from source:
   `ai-pound-shorts-vo` (the main library, ten videos), `ai-pound-shorts`,
   `ai-summit-stories`, `case-studies`, `publisher`, `tiktok-review`.
4. **One duplicate pair:** `03-Restaurant-HOOK` and `03-Restaurant-Part1-RECUT-25s` are the
   same 25.63s film, same script. The RECUT is the keeper; the HOOK is now only level-matched.
5. **`tiktok-review-demo.mp4` is 181s with speech only in the first ~18s.** Fine as a screen
   recording, not fine to post as-is.
