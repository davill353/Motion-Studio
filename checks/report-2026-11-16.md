## How to read caption accuracy

Accuracy is the share of script words found on screen in 14 sampled frames. It is
indicative, not proof: a card can fall between two samples. Any asset scoring under 90
was opened frame by frame and the text is present (skincare 4:5 and hair 4:5 both read
in full); the misses are sampling gaps, not missing captions.

## Loudness

Every mix lands between -16.6 and -15.5 LUFS. Platforms normalise to about -14, so these
sit 1 to 2 dB quiet. Not a block; a small gain lift on the next render would put them
closer to target.
