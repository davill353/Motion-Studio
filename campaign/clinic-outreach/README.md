# Free AI for every clinic: 16 Nov to 15 Dec 2026

The campaign pack for The AI Pound's local clinic and salon outreach.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `30-day-plan.md` | One video a day at 12:15: hooks, what to film, on-screen titles, closing line |
| `content-calendar-30-day-plan.csv` | The same 30 posts as calendar rows (no header row, 17 columns). All `draft`; imported to the `content-calendar-template` tab |
| `prospects-pipeline.csv` | 765 clinics and salons across 14 towns, scored A/B/C, with a first line each. Imported to the `Prospects` tab |
| `route-sheets.pdf` | Printable drive sheets, six days, top 20 independents per day |
| `flyer-clinic-pilot-A4-2up.pdf` | Leave-behind flyer, A4 with two copies per sheet (cut down the middle). QR goes to `theaipound.co.uk/pilot/?utm_source=flyer` |

## Rules the plan runs on

- Real numbers only. Never film or name a business without its permission.
- Demo content is labelled "demo build, sample data".
- Nothing publishes until a row is set to `ready`, and every post still needs Telegram approval.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour; pin the AUTOMATE comment.

## Outreach status values

`to contact`, `visited`, `interested`, `pilot`, `not interested`, `signed`. Log every visit on the Prospects tab.
