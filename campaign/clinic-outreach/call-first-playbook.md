# Call first, nothing clinics only

Decided 17 Nov 2026 after the Sandy walk-in.

## The rule
Do not compare what we build against what a clinic already runs. Comparing turns it into a war
about their past choices, and they defend the choice rather than hear the offer.

Call the clinics that have **nothing** first: no online booking, no chat widget, no booking platform.
They have no decision to defend. The only gap is that the phone and the DMs land on the person who is
mid-treatment.

No more unannounced walk-ins. A salon at 11am is somebody's place of work, and being interrupted
there costs goodwill we cannot buy back. Phone, agree it is worth 15 minutes, book a time, then turn up.

## The list
GO verdict and no booking platform at all: **429 clinics**. Nearest first: Bedford 57, Stevenage 34,
Biggleswade 25, Hitchin 25, Letchworth 23, Sandy 15, St Albans 31, Welwyn Garden City 26.
282 of them are mobile numbers, which usually means the owner answers.

Working order: Sandy, Biggleswade, Letchworth, Hitchin, Stevenage, Bedford, then further out.

## When to ring
8.30-9.30am before the first client. 6-7pm after the last one. Never midday for a solo therapist.
Landlines: ask for the owner by name, ring back when she is free.

## The call
"Is that [name]? It's David, I'm local, I build AI receptionists for clinics. You've no online booking,
so I'd guess you're the one answering your messages between clients. Have I got that right?"

Then stop talking. If yes: "That's the bit I take off you. Can I have 15 minutes this week?"
If they are with a client: "No problem, when are you quiet? I'll ring then." Never leave it as
"ring me back", it does not happen.

## What changed from the walk-in pitch
Drop the five-question test as an opening move. It was built to beat an incumbent, and these clinics
have no incumbent. Keep it for the ones that do have a platform, later, as a follow-up not a doorstep.
