# Privacy Policy: The AI Pound Publisher

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

The AI Pound Publisher is an internal tool operated by The AI Pound (UK). It publishes The AI Pound's own videos to The AI Pound's own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts.

**What it accesses.** When The AI Pound connects its accounts, the tool receives permission to upload videos and read basic channel/page information on those accounts only. It does not access anyone else's accounts.

**What it stores.** Access tokens are stored encrypted in our automation platform (n8n) so posts can be published on schedule. Post titles, captions and links to published posts are kept in our private content calendar.

**What it doesn't do.** It does not sell data, use data for advertising, or share data with third parties other than the platforms the videos are posted to.

**Website forms and leads.** If you request our free guide or a free audit on theaipound.co.uk, we collect your name, email address and anything else you choose to tell us (such as business type, phone number or message). If you comment one of our keywords (for example AUTOMATE or AUDIT) on our social media posts, we record your public username, comment and the post it was on so we can reply. We use this only to send what you asked for and to arrange calls you requested. We send marketing emails only if you tick the optional box, and you can unsubscribe at any time. Our legal basis is your consent and our legitimate interest in replying to your request (UK GDPR).

**Where it's stored and for how long.** Lead details are stored in our private Google Sheet and processed by n8n, Google and Telegram on our behalf. We keep lead details for up to 24 months, then delete them. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing us, and you can complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk).

**Google user data.** Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

**Removing access.** Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google), in Facebook/Instagram settings under Business Integrations, or in TikTok's app permissions.
