Privacy Policy: The AI Pound Publisher

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The AI Pound Publisher is an internal tool operated by The AI Pound (UK). It publishes The AI Pound's own videos to The AI Pound's own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts.

What it accesses. When The AI Pound connects its accounts, the tool receives permission to upload videos and read basic channel/page information on those accounts only. It does not access anyone else's accounts.

What it stores. Access tokens are stored encrypted in our automation platform (n8n) so posts can be published on schedule. Post titles, captions and links to published posts are kept in our private content calendar.

What it doesn't do. It does not collect personal data from the public, sell data, use data for advertising, or share data with third parties other than the platforms the videos are posted to.

Google user data. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google), in Facebook/Instagram settings under Business Integrations, or in TikTok's app permissions.
