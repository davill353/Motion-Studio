What it actually does

Answers the routine

Treatments, prices, opening hours, parking, aftercare, patch test rules. Straight from your own list, so it cannot invent an offer you do not run.

Prices in code, not in the model

Totals are calculated from your treatment sheet. The language model writes the reply, it never does the arithmetic. A wrong total is a refund and a bad review.

Stops at anything clinical

Pregnancy, breastfeeding, Roaccutane, active infection, under 18. The assistant stops, sends it to your team with the reason, and quotes no price.

Asks before it books

Every booking goes to your team to confirm until you switch that off. You keep control of the diary, the assistant just removes the typing.

How the two weeks run

  1. Day one, 45 minutes. We put your treatments, prices and rules into a sheet. That sheet is the assistant's memory.
  2. Days two to three. I connect it to a WhatsApp number and run test enquiries with you, including the ones it should refuse.
  3. Week one. It answers live, but every reply comes to your team first for a one-tap approval. You see exactly what it would have said.
  4. Week two. We release the replies you are happy with and keep the approval gate on anything clinical or unusual.
  5. Day fourteen. You keep the number of hours it saved, or we switch it off. Nothing to cancel.

What it will not do

It will not give clinical advice, diagnose, or recommend a treatment plan. It will not offer a discount you have not authorised. It will not pretend to be a human. If it does not know, it says so and passes the enquiry to your team.

Why the approval step matters. Most AI demos look good until they misread a message. This one is designed on the assumption that it will, which is why a person signs off before anything reaches a patient.
