# Outreach playbook: Sandy and surrounding towns

Goal: **3 paying clinic/salon clients by mid-December** (free 2-week pilot → £200 setup + £50/month).
Daily target: **10 conversations** (walk-ins, DMs or WhatsApp). Log every one in the **Pipeline** tab.

## Walk-in script (60 seconds)

> "Hi, I'm David, I'm local, from Sandy. Quick one, I won't keep you. Do you get lots of the same questions on WhatsApp or Instagram — prices, aftercare, 'have you got anything Thursday'?
>
> I set up an assistant that answers those in your words, from your own price list, and sends anything clinical straight to you. You approve every reply to start with.
>
> I'm running free two-week pilots for a few clinics round here. No card, no contract — if it doesn't save you time, I switch it off. Who's the best person to leave this with?"

Then: hand over the flyer, ask for the owner's name and the best number or Instagram, and log it.

**If they're interested:** book the 45-minute setup there and then ("Does Tuesday morning work?").
**If "not now":** "No problem, can I check back in a couple of weeks?" → set a follow-up date.

**Follow-up (4 days later, once only):** "Hi [name], just bumping this in case it got buried. Happy to drop by with a quick demo if easier."

## Rules (UK)

- **WhatsApp and Instagram DMs** to business accounts: fine, one-to-one, personal, with no mass sending.
- **Cold email:** OK to limited companies (always include an opt-out line). For **sole traders and partnerships** you need their consent first (PECR), so use DMs, phone or walk-ins instead.
- **Phone:** check the number isn't on the TPS/CTPS list before calling.
- Never promise results: say "designed to save time", not "will save you X hours".
- Stop contacting anyone who says no.
