# Phone script: book a 10-minute visit

**Goal of the call:** book 10 minutes to show the demo. Don't sell on the phone.
**When to call:** 9:00–10:00 or after 16:00, outside treatment hours.
**Before calling:** the number must be checked against the TPS/CTPS (do-not-call registers).

## 1. Receptionist answers
> "Hi, it's David from The AI Pound. I'm local, from Sandy. Who looks after how you handle enquiries and bookings? Is that the owner?"

- **"They're with a client":** "No problem. When's a good time to catch them? I'll ring back then." *(Write it down. Ring exactly then.)*
- **"What's it about?":** "I've got something to show them about answering customer messages after hours. It's only 10 minutes, and it's free."

## 2. Owner / manager
> "Hi [name], it's David from The AI Pound. I'm local, from Sandy, so I won't keep you long.
>
> Quick question: when you're with a client, or after you close, who answers the Instagram and WhatsApp messages asking about prices and availability?"

*(Stop. Let them answer. This is the most important part.)*

- **"Nobody / me, later / we try to":**
  > "That's what I hear from most clinics. I've built an assistant that answers those messages in your words, from your own price list, at any time of day. Anything medical goes straight to you, and you approve replies to start with.
  >
  > It's much easier to show than explain. Could I pop in for 10 minutes and let you try it on your phone? I'll set it up with your treatments first, so you'll see exactly how it would answer your customers. Would Tuesday or Thursday be better?"

- **"We already have AI / our booking system does that":**
  > "Lots of clinics do, and you don't need to change it. Can I ask: does it actually chat to customers about treatments and prices, or mainly send reminders and take bookings?
  >
  > Ours works alongside your booking system. It handles the conversation part, then sends them to your normal booking link. Nothing to switch. Worth 10 minutes to see the difference?"

## 3. Objections
| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Send me an email." | "Happy to. What's the best address? To be honest, it makes much more sense in 10 minutes on your phone. Could I pencil in a time, and you can cancel if the email puts you off?" |
| "How much is it?" | "The first two weeks are free, no card. After that it's £200 to set up and £50 a month, and you can cancel any time. But see if it's useful first." |
| "Not interested." | "No problem at all, thanks for your time. Can I ask what you use for bookings at the moment?" *(Write it down. It helps the list.)* |
| "Too busy right now." | "Totally understand. Would early January be better? I'll give you a call then." |
| "Is it a robot pretending to be us?" | "It replies in your words, and to start with you approve every reply before it goes out. Anything medical or tricky comes straight to you." |

## 4. Close
> "Brilliant, so that's [day] at [time], 10 minutes. I'll bring the demo set up with your treatments. What's the best mobile, in case I'm running late? See you then, [name]."

**After the call:** log it in the tracker: called / appointment / not now (follow-up date) / no, plus their booking system.

## 5. Voicemail (keep it under 20 seconds)
> "Hi, it's David from The AI Pound, I'm local, from Sandy. I've got something quick to show you about answering customer messages when you're busy. It's free and takes 10 minutes. I'll try you again tomorrow morning, or you can reach me on 07984 692252. Thanks."

## Rules
- No promises of results ("you'll get more bookings").
- Don't name other clinics unless they've agreed.
- If they say no, accept it straight away and stay polite. Thank them.
